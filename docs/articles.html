Simulating sea-level rise impacts on mangrove ecosystems

Denilson da Silva Bezerra · Silvio Amaral · Marcio Kampel · Paulo Roberto Andrade

The reference study introduced BR-MANGUE as a spatially explicit cellular model for exploring mangrove response to sea-level rise on Maranhão Island, Brazil. Its rules and assumptions form the scientific starting point for the Studio architecture.
